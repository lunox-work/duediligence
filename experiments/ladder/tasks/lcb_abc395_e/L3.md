# Flip Edge

In a directed graph, you start at the first vertex and want to reach the last vertex. Each step, you can either move along an outgoing edge for a cost of 1, or reverse the direction of every edge in the graph for a fixed cost X. Find the minimum total cost to reach the last vertex.

Input

The input is given from Standard Input in the following format:
N M X
u _ 1 v _ 1
u _ 2 v _ 2
\vdots
u _ M v _ M

Output

Print the minimum total cost required to reach vertex N.
