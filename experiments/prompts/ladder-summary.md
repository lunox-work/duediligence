`statement.md` is a programming problem's statement. Write `summary.md`:
two or three plain sentences saying what the program must compute, for a
reader who will not see the statement.

- Say what is asked, not how to solve it.
- Name no constraints or limits, give no examples, and leave out edge
  cases and special conditions.
- Do not describe the input or output format; the reader gets that
  separately.
- Write nothing but `summary.md`, and nothing in it but the sentences.
